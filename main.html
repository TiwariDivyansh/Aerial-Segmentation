AI-based automated urban parcel mapping and cadastral feature extraction from drone imagery — turning raw segmentation output into a validated, GIS-ready handoff.

What our approach does differently

Three specialised models, not one generalist

A land-use classifier, a building-vs-parcel separator, and a dedicated boundary-delineation model — each trained for its own task instead of one model doing everything.

Automated topology validation

Generated parcels are checked against neighbouring parcels and existing GIS land records for overlaps, gaps, and mismatches before being marked ready for use.

Confidence-based surveyor routing

Every parcel carries a confidence score. Low-confidence areas are automatically flagged and sent to a field surveyor instead of being accepted as correct.

A feedback loop that keeps learning

Surveyor corrections feed back into retraining, so accuracy improves with real-world use instead of staying fixed after the first training run.

Vision models and government drone programmes already prove feature extraction and data collection work on their own. What's missing is the bridge from raw AI output to a legacy GIS workflow a land-records officer can trust — that's the gap this closes.
